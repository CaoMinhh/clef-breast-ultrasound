Evaluate the breast ultrasound image using lesion morphology,
margin, orientation, echogenicity, posterior acoustic features,
and surrounding tissue appearance.

Benign:
Features predominantly compatible with a benign lesion,
such as an oval/round shape, circumscribed margin,
and parallel orientation.

Malignant:
Features predominantly suspicious for malignancy,
such as irregular shape, non-circumscribed margin,
non-parallel orientation, or suspicious posterior acoustic features.
